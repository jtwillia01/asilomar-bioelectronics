Mission

Modern electronics and materials science are bringing revolutionary advances to biointerface design and are shattering the limits of what is possible in the areas of biomedical diagnostics and sensing, neuroscience, and prosthetics.

Bioelectronics seeks to explore the interfaces between electronics, materials science and other engineering disciplines with biochemistry, biophysics, and general biology. This interactive symposium focuses on defining the frontiers of bioelectronics and mapping future directions. The collegial environment of this symposium will allow leading experts in the field, rising stars, and novices to discuss a broad array of topics.

About ABS

First organized in 2016, the Asilomar Bioelectronics Symposium now runs every two years. It is a small meeting (<70 participants) by design. The intimate setting, the Pacific Grove/Monterey environment, and its activities encourage discussion and engagement, both between participants and across career stages. Talks and posters take place in the morning and after dinner, with afternoons open. Presentations are by invitation only, with poster presenter slots contributed by both researchers and trainees. ABS operates outside of professional societies or organizations, and relies on registration and sponsorship to sustain itself.
